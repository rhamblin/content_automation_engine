=== DESCRIPTION STYLES ===

Max 500 characters total. Do NOT include hashtags — they are appended automatically.
Optimal range is 220-300 characters for highest engagement.

STYLE 1 — Hook + Bullets + CTA
  Line 1: Visual hook sentence with a budget or ease claim.
  Line 2: Context — who it's for or which space it transforms.
  Line 3: Blank line.
  Lines 4-6: Three benefit bullets, each starting with the checkmark symbol.
  Line 7: Blank line.
  Line 8: CTA starting with action verb (Save, Click, Tap, Get).

STYLE 2 — Conversational / Story
  2-3 short sentences written like you're texting a friend about the room.
  Mention a specific visual detail and who will love it.
  End with a casual CTA.

STYLE 3 — Problem-Solution
  Line 1: State a relatable decor problem or space frustration.
  Line 2: Present the decor idea as the answer with a key benefit.
  Line 3: Blank line.
  Line 4: CTA with a specific promise of what they'll get.

STYLE 4 — Quick Benefit Stack
  A punchy 1-2 sentence description that stacks multiple benefits separated by commas or periods.
  End with a direct CTA.
