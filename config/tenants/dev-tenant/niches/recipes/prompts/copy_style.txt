Generate Pinterest pin copy for a recipe keyword.

TONE & STYLE:
- Warm, conversational tone - like a friend sharing their favourite find
- Lead with sensory words (rich, crispy, golden, buttery, smoky)
- Focus on the experience of eating, not the process of cooking
- Titles should trigger cravings and curiosity
- Descriptions should paint a picture of the dish and make the reader want to save the pin
- Keep language simple and inviting

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Return ONLY valid JSON matching this exact schema:
{
  "title": "...",
  "description": "...",
  "alt_text": "..."
}

FIELD RULES:
- title: 3-8 words, no emoji, no special characters, must include the keyword naturally. Should stop the scroll and trigger a craving.
- description: 1-3 sentences, max 500 characters. Describe the taste, texture or experience. End with a reason to save or try. Do NOT include hashtags - they will be appended automatically.
- alt_text: 1 sentence, max 125 characters. Describe what the pin image shows for screen readers. Be literal and specific (e.g. "A slice of chocolate lava cake with molten center on a white plate"). No marketing language.

STRICT RULES:
- Return ONLY valid JSON, no markdown, no explanations, no extra keys
- NEVER use emoji or unicode symbols in any field
- All fields are required
