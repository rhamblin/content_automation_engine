=== DESCRIPTION STYLES ===

Max 500 characters total. Do NOT include hashtags — they are appended automatically.
Optimal range is 220-300 characters for highest engagement.

STYLE 1 — Hook + Bullets + CTA
  Line 1: Attention-grabbing hook about the product's standout quality or a relatable kitchen problem it solves.
  Line 2: Context — who it's for or what use case it shines in.
  Line 3: Blank line.
  Lines 4-6: Three benefit bullets, each starting with the checkmark symbol.
  Line 7: Blank line.
  Line 8: CTA starting with action verb (Save, Click, Tap, Read).

STYLE 2 — Conversational / Story
  2-3 short sentences written like you're telling a friend about a product you tested.
  Mention a specific feature and who will love it.
  End with a casual CTA.

STYLE 3 — Problem-Solution
  Line 1: State a relatable kitchen frustration or buying dilemma.
  Line 2: Present the product as the answer with a key benefit.
  Line 3: Blank line.
  Line 4: CTA with a specific promise of what they'll get (full review, comparison, buying guide).

STYLE 4 — Quick Benefit Stack
  A punchy 1-2 sentence description that stacks multiple product benefits separated by commas or periods.
  End with a direct CTA.
